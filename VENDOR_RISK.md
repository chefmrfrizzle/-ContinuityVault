# Vendor risk

Providers: Vercel, Clerk, Neon, Upstash, Resend, Twilio, Stripe, and the selected monitoring/analytics services. Each integration must be scoped, privacy-reviewed, contract-reviewed, configured separately by environment, and tested for outage/failure behavior. Notification providers receive neutral minimum content. No provider signal alone authorizes release. A secondary notification route and provider-exit plans remain required before pilot.

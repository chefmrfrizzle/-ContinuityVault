# Incident response

1. Activate the global release freeze and preserve evidence without protected content.
2. Classify scope: authentication, integrity, workflow, provider, tenant isolation, or confidentiality.
3. Open redacted operator cases and notify assigned incident roles through verified channels.
4. Do not resume delivery while evidence is uncertain or systems disagree.
5. Contain, rotate affected credentials, patch, validate audit chains, and test restore/reconciliation.
6. Remove the freeze only through documented two-person control after security approval.
7. Notify affected parties and authorities as legally required; complete a blameless review.

Incident roles, paging provider, and jurisdictional deadlines must be assigned before pilot.

# Operator access

Operators see redacted plan/workflow state, delivery status codes, and exception summaries. They may freeze, extend a policy-authorized hold, contact an owner through verified channels, and record a resolution. They cannot read plaintext or keys, change envelopes, force delivery, skip quorum, shorten a final hold, or conceal events. Internal routes require a separate role, strong step-up authentication, least privilege, session logging, and two-person control for freeze removal.
